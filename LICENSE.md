## No warranty, and specifically no accuracy claim

The capitals are below, as usual. This part is in plain words because it is the
part people actually need.

This Software changes the text of a site's content in bulk, at the request of
the person who runs it. It shows every change before it is made and keeps what
it needs to put changes back, but a search pattern that matches more than its
author meant will change more than its author meant. Reading the preview before
applying it is the safeguard, and it is the person applying the sweep who reads
it.

Undo restores a field only where nobody has edited it since the sweep, and the
history it relies on is kept for a limited time. Neither is a substitute for the
site's own backups or version control.

The Software does not create redirects, and changing text that appears in a URL
can break links to the site. Deciding whether a change is correct, safe for the
site's links, and allowed by whoever owns the content is work for a person.
